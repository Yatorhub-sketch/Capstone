Admission Requirements

  • Valid national ID or passport
  • Certified copies of secondary school leaving certificates
  • Two passport-sized photographs
  • Proof of English proficiency (where applicable)
  • Completed and signed application form
  • Payment of the registration fee receipt

Registration Steps

  1. Gather all required admission documents listed above.
  2. Fill in every field of the registration form completely.
  3. Review your details and accept the Terms & Conditions.
  4. Click Submit Registration to send your application.
  5. Check your email inbox for a confirmation message.
  6. Report to the admissions office with your original documents within 5 working days.
